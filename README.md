## My favorite series

### Mindhunter

![Mindhunter series banner](https://ntvb.tmsimg.com/assets/p14413981_b_h10_ab.jpg?w=1280&h=720)

Mindhunter is an American psychological crime thriller series on Netflix that ran for two seasons (19 episodes) from October 13, 2017, to August 16, 2019. Created by Joe Penhall and executive produced by David Fincher and Charlize Theron, it is based on the 1995 true-crime book *Mindhunter: Inside the FBI's Elite Serial Crime Unit* by John E. Douglas and Mark Olshaker.
